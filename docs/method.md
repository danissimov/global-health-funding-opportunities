# How this list was built

[Back to the list](../README.md)

## Scope

- **Countries:** the ten partner countries on the Global Health Academy map: Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Armenia, India, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines, Uganda, Zimbabwe and Botswana.
- **Who it is for:** clinicians, nurses, public-health staff and faculty who do research alongside clinical work, often with small budgets and no grants office.
- **What counts:** research grants, fellowships, training in kind, small grants, travel awards, free compute, and journal access or fee waivers.
- **Date checked:** 26 September 2026.

## Steps

1. **Five research passes, run in parallel.** Each covered one kind of funder: national funders in each country; multilaterals, regional bodies and development banks; foreign governments and research councils; foundations and companies (including AI); fellowships, small grants and search tools.
2. **Each pass checked funder pages.** For every candidate it recorded the amount, duration, eligible countries, who can apply, the current round, how to apply and the pages used.
3. **Merge and clean.** Duplicates were merged, keeping the most detailed and most recent entry. Statuses were grouped into six labels.
4. **Second check of the key facts.** World Bank income groups (FY2027), Wellcome's country list for rounds opening 29 October 2026, and the US bilateral agreements (KFF tracker, data as of 11 September 2026) were checked again on the primary pages.
5. **Curation.** Each entry was rated for fit with the audience. Entries rated a poor fit stay in the data but not in the list.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | A round is open now. The closing date is shown where known. |
| Open all year | Applications are accepted at any time, or reviewed monthly. |
| Closed now; next round expected | The last round closed, and the funder runs it regularly. |
| Status not confirmed | The programme exists, but we could not verify the current round. |
| By invitation or through a local network | No open call. Access is by invitation, or through a club or partner. |
| Paused | The funder has paused the scheme. |

## Limits

- The web search budget ran out partway through. Some entries, mostly smaller European and Asian donors, rely on the funder's main page only.
- The Dominican Republic and Jamaica entries come from direct page checks and are thinner than the others.
- Funding policy is changing quickly in 2026, especially in the US and the UK. A status can change the week after it was checked.
- The "fit" rating is a judgement for clinician-researchers with small projects. A low-fit scheme can still be right for you.
