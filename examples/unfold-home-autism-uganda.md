# UnFold-Home: caregiver-led visual learning for young children with autism in Kampala

> Example project for the plenary. It builds on the conference poster *"Unfold: Autism-Friendly Visual Learning Platform for Inclusive Early Childhood Development"* (Nakibuule, Nakanwagi, Matovu, Luboga; St. Stephen's Hospital Mpererwe, Uganda). The research framing below is a proposed next step, not the authors' plan.

| Field | Value |

## Problem

- Autism affects about 1 in 100 children worldwide (WHO). In Uganda most are diagnosed late, and specialist therapy is scarce and concentrated in Kampala.
- Families are the main therapists by default, with little guidance or material in their language.
- Schools and health workers lack simple tools to support early communication.

## Research question

Is a caregiver-delivered visual learning app feasible and acceptable for families of young children with autism in Kampala, and does it show a signal of improved communication and caregiver confidence over 16 weeks?

## What we will do (12 months)

1. **Co-design (months 1–3).** Needs assessment with 3 communities; 2 co-design workshops with caregivers, teachers and health workers; ethics and data-protection approvals.
2. **Build the MVP (months 3–6).** 50 visual modules; offline and low-bandwidth; Luganda and English. AI used for rule-based personalisation of the next module, not for diagnosis. No child identifiers leave the phone.
3. **Pilot (months 6–11).** 40 children with a confirmed diagnosis; 16 weeks of caregiver-led use; 10 teachers and health workers trained. Single-arm pre–post design with a 3-month waiting-list comparison if numbers allow.
4. **Report and plan (month 12).** Feasibility benchmarks and a protocol for a controlled trial.

## Outcomes

| Type | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Feasibility | Recruitment, weekly use (target ≥ 3 sessions/week), retention at 16 weeks |
| Communication (exploratory) | A caregiver-reported communication scale validated or adapted for Uganda (choose in co-design) |
| Caregiver | Confidence and stress scales; focus groups |
| Health workers | Knowledge before and after training |
| Safety | Distress events, privacy incidents |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Too few diagnosed children | Recruit through the hospital's paediatric clinic and special-needs schools; accept 25 as a minimum |
| Families without smartphones | Lend tablets; offline mode |
| Outcome tools not validated locally | Pick and adapt tools during co-design; report as exploratory |
| Stigma | Community awareness sessions; careful wording in recruitment |

## Sustainability

Free app for families; schools and NGOs pay a small licence or the Ministry of Education adopts it for inclusive early-childhood classes. Content is open so others can translate it.

## Ready-to-paste summaries

**One sentence.** An offline, Luganda-language visual learning app that lets caregivers in Kampala deliver daily early-communication practice to young children with autism, tested for feasibility in a 40-child pilot.

**50 words.** Young children with autism in Uganda wait years for therapy that rarely comes. Families are the therapists by default but have no tools in their language. We will co-design an offline visual learning app with caregivers, teachers and health workers, and test whether families use it and communication improves.
