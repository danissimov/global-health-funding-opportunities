## National and domestic funders

Full details, because these are the most likely first grants.

#### [Makerere University Research and Innovations Fund](https://rif.mak.ac.ug/rif8-call-for-applications-fy-2026-27/)

*Government of Uganda via Makerere University (Mak-RIF)* · Up to UGX 150m per award (~USD 41k) · Per call guidelines (FY2026/27)  
`best fit`

- **What it funds:** Research and innovation linked to the national Research and Innovation Agenda (seven priority thematic areas, incl. health).
- **Who can apply:** PI/Co-PI must be appointed Makerere academic or research staff (incl. College of Health Sciences / Mulago-affiliated faculty). Non-Makerere clinicians can join as collaborators.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Open - closes 2 Oct 2026 (days after the conference). Deadline: Opened 31 Aug 2026; closes Fri 2 Oct 2026, 23:59 EAT.
- **How to apply:** Register on rif.mak.ac.ug/portal; read full call at rif.mak.ac.ug/rif-8-call; questions to info.rif@mak.ac.ug.

#### [STI Secretariat (Office of the President) R&D and Innovation grants / Innovation Fund](https://sti.go.ug/innovation-fund/)

*Science, Technology and Innovation Secretariat - Office of the President (STI-OP)*  
`AI`

- **What it funds:** R&D and innovation along prioritised value chains, incl. health products, diagnostics, pharmaceuticals and indigenous medicine commercialisation.
- **Who can apply:** Scientists and innovators in academia, MDAs and private sector; product/commercialisation orientation.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain - no confirmed open call found for late 2026. Deadline: Periodic.
- **How to apply:** Monitor sti.go.ug and university research offices for STI-OP calls.

#### [Uganda National Health Research Organisation (UNHRO) and constituent institutes](https://unhro.org.ug/)

*UNHRO (with UVRI, JCRC, NCRI, TDRI, UMRC)*

- **What it funds:** Statutory steward of health research; constituent institutes (e.g., UVRI) host partnerships and training that can support local investigators.
- **Who can apply:** Collaboration route for clinicians wanting lab or data partnerships.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain - no open grant scheme identified. Deadline: None identified.
- **How to apply:** Approach institute departments for collaboration/co-investigatorship.
