Botswana is upper-middle income but still on the Wellcome and NIHR lists. It is not an EDCTP member, so Botswana partners usually cannot receive EDCTP3 money. The Harvard and Penn partnerships are the practical way in.

## Best first moves

1. **[University of Botswana Staff Internal Funding Rounds](https://www.ub.bw/research/funding-opportunities)**. University of Botswana internal funding rounds.
2. **[Botswana-Harvard (BHP) and Botswana-UPenn (BUP) partnerships](https://bhp.org.bw/training)**. Join studies at Botswana-Harvard or Botswana-Penn.
3. **[NIHR Global Advanced Fellowship (and NIHR Global Research Professorship)](https://www.nihr.ac.uk/career-development/research-career-funding-programmes/postdoctoral/global-advanced-fellowship)**. NIHR still funds sub-Saharan Africa.
4. **[Wellcome Early-Career Awards](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/schemes/wellcome-early-career-awards)**. Africa stays eligible.
5. **[Commonwealth Master's, PhD and Distance Learning Scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/about-us/scholarships-and-fellowships)**. Master's closes 20 October 2026.

## National and domestic funders

Full details, because these are the most likely first grants.

#### [University of Botswana Staff Internal Funding Rounds](https://www.ub.bw/research/funding-opportunities)

*University of Botswana Office of Research and Development (ORD)* · BWP 50,000 (~USD 3.7k) early-career  
`best fit`

- **What it funds:** Seed funding for data collection, analysis and dissemination in line with national priorities (incl. Faculty of Medicine/School of Nursing research).
- **Who can apply:** Full-time UB academic staff; no external collaborators; no equipment or professional staff costs; RAs allowed.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain - check UB funding page. Deadline: Annual rounds (Round 46 ran 21 Jun-19 Jul 2024); 2026 round timing not confirmed.
- **How to apply:** Submit online via UB Converis RMS; enquiries researchfunding@ub.ac.bw.

#### [Botswana-Harvard (BHP) and Botswana-UPenn (BUP) partnerships](https://bhp.org.bw/training)

*BHP; Botswana-UPenn Partnership (with MoH and UB)*

- **What it funds:** Not open grant schemes; provide mentorship, training and co-investigator roles for Batswana early-career investigators.
- **Who can apply:** Local clinicians/nurses via MoH or UB links.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling (partnership-based). Deadline: N/A.
- **How to apply:** Contact BHP training office or BUP research team about mentored projects.

#### [Botswana National Research Fund / National Research and Innovation Council](https://www.rims.gov.bw/)

*Government of Botswana (Ministry of Communications and Innovation; SGCI-supported)*

- **What it funds:** Solution-oriented national research; NRIC Bill creates a National Research and Development Fund and a National Innovation Commercialisation and Enterprise Development Fund.
- **Who can apply:** Expected to cover researchers at public institutions; details pending.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Expected - NRIC Bill 2026 approved 8 Jun 2026 and before Parliament; first calls pending. Deadline: Not yet announced.
- **How to apply:** Monitor rims.gov.bw research portal and ministry announcements.

#### [BIUST funding opportunities (research office)](https://www.biust.ac.bw/funding-opportunities/)

*Botswana International University of Science & Technology*  
`AI`

- **What it funds:** Institutional research funding and curated external opportunities; strengths in engineering, data science and biosciences relevant to health tech/AI.
- **Who can apply:** BIUST staff; clinicians can partner for health-tech projects.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain. Deadline: Not stated.
- **How to apply:** See BIUST funding opportunities page; contact research office.
