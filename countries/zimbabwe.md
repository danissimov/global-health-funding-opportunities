## National and domestic funders

Full details, because these are the most likely first grants.

#### [Research Council of Zimbabwe (RCZ) competitive calls incl. NFRF/IDRC 2026 Joint Initiative](https://www.rcz.ac.zw/)

*Research Council of Zimbabwe (with SGCI, IDRC, Canada NFRF)* · Joint Initiative: CAD 125k-250k per project (~USD 90k-180k) depending on country · Up to 3 years  
`AI`

- **What it funds:** Managed calls (SGCI-supported) and co-funding of Zimbabwean teams in the 2026 International Joint Initiative on disruptive technologies for global challenges.
- **Who can apply:** Zimbabwean researchers at eligible institutions per RCZ national annex; international consortia required for Joint Initiative.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Closed to new entrants (invited full applications due 3 Nov 2026); RCZ-specific calls Uncertain. Deadline: Joint Initiative: NOI 3 Mar 2026, LOI 9 Jun 2026, full application (invited only) 3 Nov 2026.
- **How to apply:** Contact RCZ (secretary@rcz.ac.zw); watch rcz.ac.zw and SGCI announcements.

#### [National AIDS Council](https://nac.org.zw/rad.php)

*National AIDS Council of Zimbabwe*

- **What it funds:** HIV prevention, treatment and care programmes via social contracting with CSOs; NAC Research & Documentation unit generates programme evidence.
- **Who can apply:** Primarily civil-society programme grants rather than investigator research grants; research partnerships possible via NAC R&D unit.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain - no open research call found. Deadline: Not stated.
- **How to apply:** Contact NAC Research and Documentation unit to propose operational research partnerships.

#### [University of Zimbabwe Research, Innovation & Industrialisation funds](https://www.uz.ac.zw/index.php/funding-opportunities)

*University of Zimbabwe / Ministry of Higher & Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science & Technology Development*

- **What it funds:** Innovation-oriented university projects (Education 5.0), innovation hubs; UZ College of Health Sciences Research Support Centre lists funding opportunities.
- **Who can apply:** UZ staff; product/industrialisation framing favoured.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain. Deadline: Not stated.
- **How to apply:** Contact UZ Research & Innovation Directorate or UZCHS Research Support Centre.
