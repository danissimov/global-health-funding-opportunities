## National and domestic funders

Full details, because these are the most likely first grants.

#### [NHF Institutional Grants (incl. Research in Public Health)](https://www.nhf.org.jm/institutional-grants/)

*National Health Fund (NHF), Government of Jamaica* · Project-dependent; monthly progress reports required  
`best fit`

- **What it funds:** Mainly public-sector health projects: infrastructure, equipment, service-delivery improvement, public-health research, training, health promotion and illness prevention.
- **Who can apply:** Institutions only: MOHW, Regional Health Authorities, other government institutions, NGOs. Clinicians/nurses apply via their RHA/facility with head-of-organisation endorsement.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling. Deadline: Rolling; decision within 40 working days of a complete application.
- **How to apply:** Download Grant Request Form + proposal template from nhf.org.jm; get RHA Regional Director endorsement; submit to NHF Head Office, 25 Dominica Drive, Kingston 5.

#### [CHASE Fund](https://chase.org.jm/health/)

*Culture, Health, Arts, Sports and Education (CHASE) Fund (lottery proceeds)* · Project-based

- **What it funds:** Health facilities upgrades/equipment, training of health personnel, cancer prevention/detection/care, mental-health services, drug-abuse prevention programmes.
- **Who can apply:** Organisations/institutions; not a research grant but can fund programme components with evaluation.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling (active organisation in 2026). Deadline: Rolling (not verified).
- **How to apply:** Chase.org.jm/how-to-apply-to-chase.

#### [NHF Sponsorship for Healthcare Activities (research & publications, conferences)](https://www.nhf.org.jm/supporting-health-initiatives-in-your-community/)

*National Health Fund* · Event/activity-based

- **What it funds:** Sponsorship of research and publications, conferences (local and international), educational programmes and health fairs.
- **Who can apply:** Organisations/groups; include background, goals, budget breakdown, contact.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling. Deadline: Rolling; allow >=6 weeks for review.
- **How to apply:** Letter to the NHF CEO (address on NHF site) with budget and objectives.

#### [UWI Mona Office for Research and Innovation (MORI)](https://www.mona.uwi.edu/mori/)

*The University of the West Indies, Mona* · Not verified · Not verified

- **What it funds:** Grant support, quarterly funding-opportunity notifications, compliance; university internal research/publication funds (specific 2026 schemes not verified). SUNY-UWI Health Research Consortium offers collaboration.
- **Who can apply:** UWI staff (incl. UHWI consultants with UWI appointments).
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling (support office); internal grants Uncertain. Deadline: Quarterly notifications.
- **How to apply:** Contact MORI grant support; subscribe to funding notifications.
