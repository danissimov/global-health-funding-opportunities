## Best first moves

1. **[Provincial Dept of Science & Technology projects and hospital-level](https://www.most.gov.vn/)**. Hospital and provincial projects: the lowest barrier.
2. **[NAFOSTED Basic Research grants (incl. medicine & pharmacy council)](https://nafosted.gov.vn/thong-bao-tai-tro-nghien-cuu-co-ban-nam-2026-nafosted/)**. National basic-research fund.
3. **[Academy of Medical Sciences Networking Grants (ISPF-funded)](https://acmedsci.ac.uk/grants-and-programmes/grants/networking-grants)**. Up to £25,000 with a UK co-applicant.
4. **[Wellcome Early-Career Awards](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/schemes/wellcome-early-career-awards)**. South-East Asia stays eligible.
5. **[VinIF Science, Technology and Innovation Research Sponsor Program](https://vinif.org/en/sponsor-programs/scientific-technology-and-innovation-research-program/)**. Private national fund. Next round around May 2027.

## National and domestic funders

Full details, because these are the most likely first grants.

#### [Provincial Dept of Science & Technology projects and hospital-level](https://www.most.gov.vn/)

*Provincial Departments of Science and Technology (So KH&CN); hospitals/universities* · Small-moderate · ~1-2 years  
`best fit`

- **What it funds:** Provincial-level applied projects on local health problems; institution-level small projects at hospitals and medical universities.
- **Who can apply:** Staff of provincial hospitals and health departments; nurses often eligible for institution-level projects.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Rolling/annual (not independently verified for 2026). Deadline: Annual, per province/institution.
- **How to apply:** Ask your hospital's science & training department for the provincial and internal proposal calendar.

#### [VinIF Science, Technology and Innovation Research Sponsor Program](https://vinif.org/en/sponsor-programs/scientific-technology-and-innovation-research-program/)

*Vingroup Innovation Foundation (VinIF), Institute of Big Data* · VND 2-10 billion per project (~USD 77k-385k) · Multi-year (per agreement)  
`AI`

- **What it funds:** High-impact research projects; priority areas include smart health, AI, data science and biotechnology. Also domestic postdoc and postgraduate scholarships.
- **Who can apply:** PI must hold PhD, have >=3 international publications or 1 patent in past 5 years, commit >=30% time; host must be a Vietnamese S&T organisation. Outputs: 3 of 4 (product, Q1 papers, IP, MSc/PhD training).
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Active (programme continuing) - 2026 round closed; 2027 round expected ~Apr-May 2027. Deadline: 2026: submissions 1-15 May 2026; awards July 2026.
- **How to apply:** Portal oms.vinif.org; start building international co-PI links now.

#### [NAFOSTED Basic Research grants (incl. medicine & pharmacy council)](https://nafosted.gov.vn/thong-bao-tai-tro-nghien-cuu-co-ban-nam-2026-nafosted/)

*National Foundation for Science and Technology Development (NAFOSTED), Ministry of Science and Technology* · Typically 2-3 years

- **What it funds:** Investigator-led basic research in natural sciences/engineering (includes medicine & pharmacy council) and social sciences/humanities; separate applied-research programme.
- **Who can apply:** PI with publication track record at a Vietnamese S&T organisation (universities, research institutes, some hospitals).
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Closed - results pending; next call expected mid-2027. Deadline: 2026 basic research call closed 13 Aug 2026 (17:00); results expected before 15 Oct 2026.
- **How to apply:** Register on STM system (stm.mst.gov.vn); check PI publication requirements.

#### [Ministry of Health ministerial-level S&T tasks (de tai cap Bo)](https://moh.gov.vn/en/danh-muc-de-tai)

*Ministry of Health of Vietnam (Administration of Science, Technology & Training)* · Typically 1-3 years

- **What it funds:** Health research topics set by MoH priority lists, including clinical, public-health and health-policy studies.
- **Who can apply:** Applicants from MoH-affiliated hospitals, institutes and medical universities.
- **Status on 26 September 2026:** Uncertain - lists for 2027 implementation expected; confirm via MoH portal. Deadline: Annual; topic lists approved (e.g., March 2025 decisions for 2026 implementation).
- **How to apply:** Submit task proposals via your hospital/university science office to MoH in the annual proposal window.
