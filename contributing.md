# Contribution guidelines

Thank you for helping. This list only works if it stays correct.

## The quickest way: open an issue

You do not need to know GitHub. [Open an issue](https://github.com/danissimov/awesome-global-health-funding/issues/new/choose) and choose:

- **New opportunity**, to suggest a grant, fellowship or programme.
- **Something is out of date**, for a closed call, a new deadline, a broken link, or a change in eligibility.

## Pull requests

### What belongs here

- Funding that people in at least one of the ten countries can apply for, or join as a named investigator.
- Suitable for health workers doing research alongside clinical work: small grants, fellowships, training, or larger schemes where they can lead or join.
- With an official page that states the eligibility.

### What does not belong

- Funders that only accept invited proposals. Add them to *Not direct routes* instead.
- Loans, paid services or pages that ask for a fee to apply.
- Links to aggregator pages instead of the funder's own page.

### How to add an entry

1. Add it to the right section of `README.md`, and to each country page it is open to.
2. Use this format, one line per entry:

   ```md
   - [Scheme name](https://official-page) - `best fit` What it funds, in one sentence. Amount. Countries. *Status.*
   ```

3. Start the description with a capital letter and end every part with a full stop.
4. Keep the section order: best fit first, then by status.
5. Add a row to `data/funding_opportunities.csv` with the same facts.
6. In the pull request, paste the link where you checked the eligibility and the date you checked it.

### Style

- Plain English. Short sentences. Many readers use English as a second language.
- Write amounts with the currency: US$15,000, £25,000, UGX 150 million.
- Write dates in full: 3 December 2026.
